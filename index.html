The Hope Connection

No judgement, real hope — for wherever you are in life.

After 30+ years of drug addiction, Jesus met Lewis Fuller where he was and set him free. Now he shares honest Bible teaching on hope, faith, and recovery — meeting others where they are in the Name of Jesus. Whether you are weary, wandering, or wondering, come as you are, and Lord willing, leave anchored.

Watch: what is The Hope Connection?

About Lewis

For more than 30 years, Lewis Fuller lived in drug addiction — rehabs, meetings, nothing held. Then, reading John 17 in a men's discipleship program, he believed God's Word was true, and Jesus set him free then and there. Hear his testimony →

Watch: Lewis's testimony

From chains to Christ — the prison he built for himself, and the ones the state built for him, to the man Jesus made him. If He can set Lewis free, He can set you free too.
