Our Mission

"Meeting others where they are in the Name of Jesus."

The Hope Connection exists to make Jesus famous — not to make us famous. No judgement, real hope to wherever you are in life. Whether you are weary, wandering, or wondering, or you are looking for honest encouragement, this is the place to be. You will be met with grace and Truth in the Name of Jesus. Come as you are, and Lord willing, leave anchored.

This is a personal ministry of Lewis Fuller — not affiliated with any church, denomination, or organization.

About Lewis

For more than 30 years, Lewis Fuller lived in drug addiction — rehabs, meetings, nothing held. Then, reading John 17 in a men's discipleship program, he believed God's Word was true, and Jesus set him free then and there. Hear his testimony →
