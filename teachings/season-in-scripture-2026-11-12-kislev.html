This Season in Scripture — A Fire in the Winter House

A brazier in the winter house.

Scripture sets Jeremiah's scroll in this month: “Now the king was sitting in his winter house in the ninth month, and a fire was burning in the fire pot before him” (Jeremiah 36:22, ESV). Through their eyes, you feel why — Kislev brings the heaviest rains, cold down from snow-capped Hermon, and dark by late afternoon. Ordinary homes have no winter house, only a courtyard brazier everyone crowds close to, cloaks pulled tight.

Two days ahead.

Scripture dates a word to Zechariah to the fourth day of this month (Zechariah 7:1) — a question about fasting, asked by men who traveled to Jerusalem in the wet season.

Through their eyes, with the flocks.

Around this season sowing still runs in the first half of Kislev, but when rain hammers down, work turns to shelter. A shepherd in the Bethlehem hills coaxes sheep into a cave fold, counts them in by touch, and listens to wadis rushing that were dust weeks ago. At home, children learn Torah by repeating it after their father beside the lamp (Deuteronomy 6:7), and supper is barley bread and lentils over coals.

What is coming.

Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 22 days away, on 25 Kislev. Households keep checking lamps and their stored oil for those dark nights.

Friend, draw close to the fire God gives, speak His Word at your own table, and let Him warm what the cold season has chilled.
