Hope for Today — Issue 2

God Is With You

Isaiah 41:10

Isaiah 41:10

“Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God; I will strengthen you, I will help you, I will uphold you with my righteous right hand.”

— Isaiah 41:10 (ESV)

Reflection

Fear usually shows up before the facts do — in the night, in the waiting room, in the moment you realize you cannot fix what is in front of you. God does not scold His people for that fear; He answers it with Himself: “I am with you... I am your God.” He does not promise to explain everything, but He promises to strengthen you, to help you, and to hold you up with His own righteous right hand. So you do not have to hold yourself up today. The hand underneath you is His, and it does not shake.
