He Is There — Even When You Can't Feel Him

“Just because you can't feel the presence of God doesn't mean He is not there.”

Feelings rise and fall. They were never meant to carry the weight of your faith. God's nearness is not a feeling to be chased — it is a fact to be believed, because He said it Himself.

“The LORD is near to all who call on him, to all who call on him in truth.” — Psalm 145:18 (ESV)

Near. Not far off, not waiting for you to feel something first. Near to all who call. And the promise underneath it is iron:

“I will never leave you nor forsake you.” — Hebrews 13:5 (ESV)

If you are in a season where God feels far away, you are not forgotten and you are not alone. Call on Him — He is nearer than your feelings are telling you.

— LEWIS FULLER · THE HOPE CONNECTION
