This Season in Scripture

Every day, we open the Bible’s calendar and ask a simple question: what was God doing in this season of the year, in Bible times? It’s like the history behind the story. A flood beginning, a temple finished, rains prayed for over fresh-plowed fields — the same God, keeping His appointments with His people in every season. Come back daily; there is always something He was doing right about now.

Entries

November 8, 2026 — The Week Begins — and Eyes Turn Toward Jerusalem
28 Cheshvan: The first morning of the week — Sabbath rest is over, and tonight, at sundown, Sigd begins, with eyes turned toward Jerusalem. Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 26 days away.

November 7, 2026 — Dry Ground at Last
27 Cheshvan: Scripture dates the earth dried out to this day (Genesis 8:14) — Noah goes out onto dry ground, and a village keeps a Sabbath of rest.

November 6, 2026 — Ready Before the Sun Goes Down
26 Cheshvan: the sun sets early over the hill country, and by mid-afternoon every village from Hebron to Galilee is hurrying — bread baked, lamps trimmed, and animals brought in before Sabbath. Genesis 24:63.

November 5, 2026 — Kindness at the Well in the Rainy Season
25 Cheshvan: at the well at evening, Rebekah draws water for Abraham's servant and his camels — kindness offered fully, bucket after bucket. Genesis 24:19.

November 4, 2026 — The House Finished in the Month of Rains
24 Cheshvan: Solomon's Temple was finished in Bul, the rainy month — and in that same season a family rolls its flat roof flat again before the heavier rains come. 1 Kings 6:38.

November 3, 2026 — The Seed in the Fold of His Cloak
23 Cheshvan: the sower goes out with seed in the fold of his cloak, the wadis begin to run, and a family sows in faith.

November 2, 2026 — The Shepherds Come Down From the Hills
22 Cheshvan: shepherds bring their flocks down from the cold hills, cisterns fill again, and oil is stored for winter lamps.

November 1, 2026 — The Rain Prayer and the Fresh-Plowed Field
21 Cheshvan: the quiet month with no feast, the daily prayer for rain, and farmers plowing after the early rain — God at work in ordinary days.
