This Season in Scripture

Every day, we open the Bible’s calendar and ask a simple question: what was God doing in this season of the year, in Bible times? It’s like the history behind the story. A flood beginning, a temple finished, rains prayed for over fresh-plowed fields — the same God, keeping His appointments with His people in every season. Come back daily; there is always something He was doing right about now.
