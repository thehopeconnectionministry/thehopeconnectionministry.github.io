This Season in Scripture

Every day, we open the Bible’s calendar and ask a simple question: what was God doing in this season of the year, in Bible times? It’s like the history behind the story. A flood beginning, a temple finished, rains prayed for over fresh-plowed fields — the same God, keeping His appointments with His people in every season. Come back daily; there is always something He was doing right about now.

Entries

November 25, 2026 — Ten Days to Light, a Night at the Jabbok — In the Month of Kislev
15 Kislev: Around this season the Torah reading is Vayishlach — Jacob coming home after twenty years, wrestling alone at the Jabbok until dawn (Genesis 32:10, ESV), Kislev is late sowing on the Gezer calendar, and Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 9 days away.

November 24, 2026 — God Has Seen — A Son in the Month of Kislev
14 Kislev: Tradition places Reuben's birth on 14 Kislev — Leah, unloved and aching, bears Jacob's first son and names him from her hope (Genesis 29:32, ESV), fishermen work between winter storms on the Sea of Galilee, and Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 10 days away.

November 23, 2026 — A Shepherd's Cold Morning in the Month of Kislev
13 Kislev: Nehemiah's burden came in this month — Nehemiah hears that Jerusalem's walls lie broken (Nehemiah 1:1, ESV), shepherds move their flocks to lower ground before the worst cold, and Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 11 days away.

November 22, 2026 — Back to the Loom on the First Day in the Month of Kislev
12 Kislev: Back to work on the first day — the Sabbath lamp has burned out, the loom is set, and “establish the work of our hands upon us; yes, establish the work of our hands!” (Psalm 90:17, ESV).

November 21, 2026 — Sabbath Rest in the Month of Kislev
11 Kislev: Sabbath in the short days — by Friday afternoon the sowing stops, the market empties, bread is baked and lamps are filled, and a family walks home in the cold light, and Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 13 days away.

November 20, 2026 — The Road to the Square in the Month of Kislev
10 Kislev: Scripture dates it ahead of us, to the twentieth day of the ninth month, when the men of Judah and Benjamin gather in Jerusalem and 'all the people sat in the open square before the house of God, trembling because of this matter and because of the heavy rain' (Ezra 10:9, ESV), and Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 14 days away.

November 19, 2026 — The Scroll in the Fire in the Month of Kislev
9 Kislev: Baruch reads Jeremiah's scroll aloud in the Temple courts (Jeremiah 36:9–10), the king cuts each column and throws it into the fire (Jeremiah 36:22–23), yet God says 'Take another scroll and write on it all the former words' (Jeremiah 36:28), and Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 15 days away.

November 18, 2026 — The Empty Barn in the Month of Kislev
8 Kislev: Haggai's dated word is coming on 24 Kislev — the seed is still in the barn, yet God says 'from this day on I will bless you' (Haggai 2:19), day laborers wait for a day's wage, and Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 16 days away.

November 17, 2026 — News From Home in the Month of Kislev
7 Kislev: news from home reaches Nehemiah in Susa (Nehemiah 1:1–3), sowers finish the late sowing of wheat and barley, and Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 17 days away.

November 16, 2026 — The Potter's Hands in the Rainy Season
6 Kislev: a potter at his wheel when rain closes the furrows (Isaiah 64:8), craftsmen and weavers earning a living indoors, and Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 18 days away.

November 15, 2026 — Down to the Warm Valley
5 Kislev: shepherds lead their flocks down to the warm Jordan valley for winter grazing (Genesis 8:22), the week begins again after rest, and Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 19 days away.

November 14, 2026 — Mercy, Not Just Fasting
4 Kislev: Scripture dates God's word to Zechariah to today (Zechariah 7:1) — the Bethel question answered with mercy, a Shabbat of rest with Toldot read, and Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 20 days away.

November 13, 2026 — The Day Before the Question
3 Kislev: men from Bethel on the wet road to Jerusalem, carrying one honest question about fasting (Zechariah 7:9), Friday preparations before sundown, and Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 21 days away.

November 12, 2026 — A Fire in the Winter House
2 Kislev: a fire burning in the king's winter house (Jeremiah 36:22), shepherds sheltering flocks as the wadis rush, and Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 22 days away.

November 11, 2026 — Isaac Sowed in That Land
1 Kislev: Isaac sowed in that land and reaped a hundredfold (Genesis 26:12) — sowing in rain-softened ground, and fishermen working the winter lake at night, and Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 23 days away.

November 10, 2026 — A New Moon Over Wet Furrows
30 Cheshvan: Rosh Chodesh Kislev begins — a new moon over wet furrows, trumpets over the offerings, and Nehemiah's news coming in Kislev. Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 24 days away.

November 9, 2026 — On a High Hill, Facing Jerusalem
29 Cheshvan: Tradition keeps Sigd today — Beta Israel families fast, climb a high hill, and face Jerusalem, renewing the covenant. Cheshvan closes tonight; Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 25 days away.

November 8, 2026 — The Week Begins — and Eyes Turn Toward Jerusalem
28 Cheshvan: The first morning of the week — Sabbath rest is over, and tonight, at sundown, Sigd begins, with eyes turned toward Jerusalem. Hanukkah begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 26 days away.

November 7, 2026 — Dry Ground at Last
27 Cheshvan: Scripture dates the earth dried out to this day (Genesis 8:14) — Noah goes out onto dry ground, and a village keeps a Sabbath of rest.

November 6, 2026 — Ready Before the Sun Goes Down
26 Cheshvan: the sun sets early over the hill country, and by mid-afternoon every village from Hebron to Galilee is hurrying — bread baked, lamps trimmed, and animals brought in before Sabbath. Genesis 24:63.

November 5, 2026 — Kindness at the Well
25 Cheshvan: at the well at evening, Rebekah draws water for Abraham's servant and his camels — kindness offered fully, bucket after bucket. Genesis 24:19.

November 4, 2026 — A Temple Finished in the Rainy Month
24 Cheshvan: Solomon's Temple was finished in Bul, the rainy month — and in that same season a family rolls its flat roof flat again before the heavier rains come. 1 Kings 6:38.

November 3, 2026 — The Sower Goes Out
23 Cheshvan: the sower goes out with seed in the fold of his cloak, the wadis begin to run, and a family sows in faith.

November 2, 2026 — Down from the Cold Hills
22 Cheshvan: shepherds bring their flocks down from the cold hills, cisterns fill again, and oil is stored for winter lamps.

November 1, 2026 — The Quiet Month
21 Cheshvan: the quiet month with no feast, the daily prayer for rain, and farmers plowing after the early rain — God at work in ordinary days.
