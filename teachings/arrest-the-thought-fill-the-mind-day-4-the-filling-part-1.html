Philippians 4 Think on These Things The Mind One-Week Study Day 4

Finally, brothers, whatever is true, whatever is honorable, whatever is just, whatever is pure, whatever is lovely, whatever is commendable, if there is any excellence, if there is anything worthy of praise, think about these things.

Philippians 4:8 (ESV)

Day 4 of “Arrest the Thought, Fill the Mind — A One-Week Study.” Thursday, November 26, 2026.

Read

Philippians 4:8 (first four qualities)

Focus

TRUE — what matches reality as God sees it. HONORABLE — what carries weight and dignity. JUST — what is right and fair by God's standard. PURE — what could be brought into the light without shame. The arrested thought leaves ground behind; these four are the first stones of the rebuilding.

One Question

Of the four, which replacement thought do you most need today — and what is it, in your own words?
