Hope for Today — Issue 4

Great Is Thy Faithfulness

Lamentations 3:22–23

Hope for Today · November 27, 2026 · Issue 4

Lamentations 3:22–23

“The steadfast love of the LORD never ceases; his mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning; great is your faithfulness.”

— Lamentations 3:22–23 (ESV)

Reflection

Jeremiah wrote these words sitting in the middle of ruin — not after everything got better, but while it was still broken all around him. That is what makes them so strong: this is not faithfulness you only sing about on the good days. The steadfast love of the Lord never ceases, even on the worst morning of your life, and His mercies do not run out — they are new every morning, waiting for you before your feet hit the floor. You may have used up yesterday. You have not used up God. Great is His faithfulness — not because your circumstances say so, but because He does.
