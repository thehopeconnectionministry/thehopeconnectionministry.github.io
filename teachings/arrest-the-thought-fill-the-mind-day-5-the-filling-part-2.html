Philippians 4 Think on These Things The Mind One-Week Study Day 5

Finally, brothers, whatever is true, whatever is honorable, whatever is just, whatever is pure, whatever is lovely, whatever is commendable, if there is any excellence, if there is anything worthy of praise, think about these things.

Philippians 4:8 (ESV)

Day 5 of “Arrest the Thought, Fill the Mind — A One-Week Study.” Friday, November 27, 2026.

Read

Philippians 4:8 (last four qualities)

Focus

LOVELY — what calls forth love and softens the heart. COMMENDABLE — what would be a credit if said out loud. EXCELLENCE — not barely-allowed, but the best. WORTHY OF PRAISE — what God Himself would applaud. The standard rises from “not sinful” to “worthy of Him.”

One Question

What have you been dwelling on lately that passes “not sinful” but fails “excellent”?
