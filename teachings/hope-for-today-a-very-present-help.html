Hope for Today — Issue 3

A Very Present Help

Psalm 46:1

Hope for Today · November 24, 2026 · Issue 3

Psalm 46:1

“God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.”

— Psalm 46:1 (ESV)

Reflection

Trouble does not ask permission — it shows up in the phone call you were not expecting, the diagnosis you did not see coming, the night everything you were holding together starts to shake. And notice what this verse does not say: it does not say God is a far-off help, or a help you have to go find when you get stronger. He is a very present help — near, right now, in the middle of the trouble itself, not just waiting on the other side of it. He is your refuge, the place you run to, and He is your strength, the strength you run on when your own is gone. So do not wait until the shaking stops to come to Him. He is already here.
