This Season in Scripture — The House Finished in the Month of Rains

Finished in Bul

Scripture dates it to this month: in Solomon's eleventh year, “in the month of Bul, which is the eighth month, the house was finished in all its parts, according to all its plans” (1 Kings 6:38, ESV). Bul was the rainy month — seven years of cutting stone and cedar ended not in summer's dust, but as clouds gathered over Jerusalem and the hill country turned damp and cold.

A quiet month, still

Around this season there is still no feast of its own in Cheshvan — that quiet is the story. The next festival, Hanukkah, begins at sundown Friday, December 4 — 30 days away — and no one is traveling yet. At home, the daily prayer for rain, begun on 7 Cheshvan, is now on every lip, morning and evening.

Through their eyes, on the rooftop

Before heavier rains come, a father and his sons climb up with a heavy stone roller and roll the flat roof flat again, patching cracks with fresh mud and clearing the channels that carry rain down to the cistern. Below, a daughter learns from her mother how to store the new olive oil and barley where damp cannot reach. If the roof fails, the whole house weeps.

Friend, God finishes what He begins — in a temple, in a rainy month, and in an ordinary house kept ready by faithful hands.
