Can I pray for you today? From his front yard this morning, Lewis prays by name for the needs people are carrying: comfort, peace, and healing for the person recovering from surgery; an eased mind for the one who didn't have a good night's rest; and for anyone struggling today, the God who meets them where they are. Today is a new day — and God is for you, not against you.

The Prayer

Good morning, y'all. This is Lewis Fuller coming to you again from my front yard. Can I pray for you today?

Most gracious Heavenly Father, thank You, Lord, for Your love, Your mercy, and Your grace, Lord. For the person who is recovering from surgery, I pray, Father, You just give him comfort and peace, Lord. And Lord, provide healing according to Your will, Lord. The person that is struggling, that didn't have a good night's rest, Lord, I pray, Father, You ease their mind. Lord, may they be dependent on You, Lord. And Lord, those who are struggling, that trust You in this day or in any other day, Lord, I pray, Father, You meet them where they are. Lord, and I pray for Your guidance, leadership, and understanding, Lord. I pray, Father, for our leaders, Lord, today, Lord, that they would make the right decisions, be wise in their actions, Lord. Lord, I pray, Father, You just bless this day, Lord — that today is a new day, Lord — and that may we look to You, the author and finisher of our faith. So Lord, we thank You and we praise You. In Jesus' name we pray. Amen.

I love you, and I want to say that I'm here for you and God is for you, not against you. God bless.
