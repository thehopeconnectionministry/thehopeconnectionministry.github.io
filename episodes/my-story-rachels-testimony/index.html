You already know her voice. Rachel Fuller is the voice at the start of every episode of The Hope Connection — the one who welcomes you in before the teaching begins. Today, for the first time, she steps out from behind the intro and tells her own story.

A first for this show

This episode is a first — and a special one. Rachel is the show's first guest host, and because the guest is the very voice that normally opens the program, this is the one episode that opens differently: Lewis introduces her himself. His words are simple and proud — today, Rachel is sharing her testimony of what the Lord has done in her life.

Rachel is Lewis's wife, his partner in this ministry, and a counselor who has spent years sitting with hurting people. This time, she is the one in the guest's chair — not asking the questions, but answering the biggest one: what has God done for you?

A testimony is not a story about how strong we were. It is a story about how faithful God is.

Why we tell our stories

Every testimony tells the truth in the same order: the bad news first, then the good news. We were lost — and the bad news never stands alone, because it always points to Christ. Rachel's story is honest about the road behind her, and it is just as honest about the God who met her on it. That is the heartbeat of this ministry: meeting others where they are in the Name of Jesus, because we know what it is to be met there ourselves.

Scripture treats a testimony as a weapon, not a memoir. Revelation says the saints conquer "by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony" — the finished work of Jesus, and the plain telling of what He has done. When Rachel tells what the Lord has done for her soul, somebody listening hears that He can do it for them too.

And there is a word for households here as well. This episode is one couple's way of saying Joshua's words out loud: "As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord." Two stories, one Savior — and one home that belongs to Him.

One simple response

Listen all the way through. Then ask yourself: what has the Lord done for my soul — and who needs to hear me say it? Somebody you know is where Rachel once was. Send them this episode. You never know whose darkest moment is right now, or whose turning point begins with a story like hers.
