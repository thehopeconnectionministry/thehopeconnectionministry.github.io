An Honest Conversation

I recently sat down with Tray Crayton on SHOUT OUT TO GOD LIVE for Episode 05, and we talked about everything — the childhood church memories, the years I felt like I didn't belong, the performance, the addiction, and the night grace broke through.

This is the full segment, unhurried. If the short clips blessed you, here's the whole story.

What We Talked About

Tray asked honest questions, and I gave honest answers: what it was like being the kid asking questions nobody would answer, the youth pastor whose words stuck with me for decades, the slow drift into thirty years of addiction, and the Scripture — John 17:3 — that changed everything.
