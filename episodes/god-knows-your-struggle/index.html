God Knows Your Struggle

He is with you. He is for you. He is not against you.

  • Prayer
  • Encouragement
  • Hope
  • Faith

God knows. Whatever you are carrying this morning — the struggle you can't find your way out of, the day you can barely get out of bed for — He knows your situation, and He has not left you alone in it. Lewis recorded this prayer for you.

The Prayer

Good morning on this cloudy morning. You see this shirt right here? It says, "God knows." You know, God knows your struggle. God knows your situation, and He's with you. He's for you. He's not against you.

And I pray, Father, right now, in the name of Jesus, for those who are struggling, those who need a word of encouragement, those who are searching for hope, Lord — that hope is found in the name of Jesus. It's found in Jesus Christ. It's found in His finished work. Lord, You said, "It is finished." Lord, it is finished. And we can rest now in Your finished work of the cross, because You are good and You make all things new.

Lord, I pray, Father, You just reach out to that person who was struggling this morning, just struggling that they can't get out of bed, they don't know which direction to turn to. Lord, I pray, Father, You just reach down, Lord, and lift them up. Lord, You make a way. You have made a way, Lord — and make a way for that person right now that just can't find their way out in their struggle.

Lord, I pray for all of you. Just, God, lead and direct. Lord, give them wisdom to use through this day. In Jesus' name, I pray. Amen.

I love you all — and God knows.
