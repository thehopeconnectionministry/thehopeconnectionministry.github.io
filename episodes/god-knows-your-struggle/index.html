He's With You in the Valley

One way in, one way out — and the way is Jesus.

  • Prayer
  • Encouragement
  • Hope
  • Faith

He is with you in the valley. Lewis lives in an actual valley — mountains on every side, one way in and one way out — and it taught him what David meant: "Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for You are with me" (Psalm 23:4). This morning's encouragement is for anyone walking through one.

The Encouragement

This is Lewis Fuller again. You know, the place I live in is a valley. And I never really understood completely what the Scripture means — "though I walk through the valley" — I didn't really get a good picture of that until I actually lived in one. I have mountains on both sides of me, the right and the left, behind me and in front of me. There's a little exit and entrance that I use to go in and out of my property. That's one way in, one way out.

It's kind of the same way when we think about "though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil." A lot of times we have that one way out — and that out is Jesus, and He is the way. One way in, one way to salvation is also through Jesus Christ.

So remember: the time when you're in the valley, He's with you. And He always provides a way out. And the way in to salvation is through Him — the finished work of the cross.

I love you. God bless you. Have a good day.
