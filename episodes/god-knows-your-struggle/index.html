Make time for God. Out in his front yard in the stillness of the morning — the fog, the quiet, and the busyness of the day already starting behind him — Lewis has a simple word for anyone feeling too busy for the Lord: prioritize your time with Him. God wants to hear from you. He wants to spend time with you. And this morning's prayer is for you.

The Encouragement

Hey again, this is Lewis Fuller, and once again I'm in my front yard. I came out here this morning and I was looking around, looking at the sky — the fog, and the stillness of the morning. And then I hear the little sounds in the background, the busyness going on — the trucks and cars going on behind me. You know, people a lot of times get too busy to spend time with the Lord. It's a good idea to prioritize yourself, because God wants to hear from you. He wants to spend time with you.

So let me say a short prayer for your encouragement: Heavenly Father, thank You, Lord, for Your love, Your mercy, Your grace. Thank You, Father, for everyone here who is looking to You, the author and the finisher of their faith. I pray, Father, You strengthen each and every one. May they continue to look to You, Lord, when they're down, when they're sad, when they're hurting — whatever they're going through, Lord, You know their need. And we thank You, Lord, for what You're going to do, and we thank You for what You're already doing. So we give You all the praise, all the glory, all the honor, in Jesus' name. Amen.

I love y'all and I thank you. God bless you. Have a blessed day.
