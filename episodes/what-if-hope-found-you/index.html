What If Hope Found You in Your Darkest Moment?

Our official commercial — and the true story behind it.

  • Hope
  • Testimony
  • Addiction Recovery
  • Grace
  • New Beginnings

What if hope found you in your darkest moment? Not after you cleaned yourself up. Not after you got it together. Right there — in the pit, in the mess, in the place you swore nobody could reach.

The pit is real

Let us be honest first. The darkest moments are not metaphors. They are the 3 a.m. kind — when you have tried everything, lost almost everything, and the mirror shows you someone you barely recognize. Rehabs, meetings, promises made and broken, resolutions that did not survive the week. For more than thirty years, that was the rhythm: nothing held.

Here is the hard truth Scripture will not let us dodge: we cannot save ourselves. Left to ourselves, we sink. The pit is real, and the chains are real, and no amount of trying harder breaks them. If that is where you are today — weary, wandering, wondering whether anything can actually change — you are exactly who this is for.

Jesus doesn't wait for you to climb out of the pit. He climbs in.

Hope has a name

On one ordinary night in a men's discipleship program, reading the words of John 17, everything changed. "And this is eternal life, that they know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom you have sent" (John 17:3, ESV). Believing God's Word was true — right then, right there — Jesus met a broken man where he was and set him free. Not after the cleanup. In the middle of the mess.

That is what the gospel does. "He drew me up from the pit of destruction, out of the miry bog, and set my feet upon a rock" (Psalm 40:2, ESV). "If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come" (2 Corinthians 5:17, ESV). Thirty-plus years of addiction did not get the final word. Jesus did.

And here is why this matters for you: the same Jesus who reached that far down can reach you. "May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, so that by the power of the Holy Spirit you may abound in hope" (Romans 15:13, ESV). Hope is not a wish that things get better. Hope is a Person who came down into the dark to bring you out.

Watch it. Share it.

This commercial is our invitation to you — just under a minute, in Lewis's own voice, carrying the whole message of this ministry: no judgement, real hope, for wherever you are in life. Whether you are weary, wandering, or wondering, come as you are, and Lord willing, leave anchored.

If this encouraged you, send it to one person who needs hope today. You never know whose darkest moment is right now.
