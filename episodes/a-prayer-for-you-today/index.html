A Prayer for You Today

We all need Jesus.

We all need Jesus. Lewis recorded this prayer for whoever is struggling right now and doesn't know which direction to go.

The Prayer

"You know, we all need Jesus. We all do — lost, saved. But there's somebody out there right now that needs a special touch from the Lord. I pray, Father God, for whoever's out there, Lord — they are struggling, don't know which direction to go, Lord. I pray, Father, in the name of Jesus, that the Holy Spirit will give them guidance, give them clarity, give them peace of mind. And I pray, Father, that Thy will be done in the name of Jesus, that You will be lifted up, that You will be glorified. And I pray, Father, that You will lead and direct all those who are in authority — Lord, our government — I pray, Father, that You will work in a mighty way. Lord, I pray, Father, for all those families that are broken, that are in their own prison. Lord, I pray, Father, You just touch each and every family out there — those who are sick, those who are struggling. Lord, You know their need. And I pray, Father, in the name of Jesus, You meet them where they are, in Jesus' name. Father, we know that You are good, Lord, and You are for us and not against us. So we thank You, Lord. In Jesus' name I pray, Amen. God bless you, and I hope this encourages you."
