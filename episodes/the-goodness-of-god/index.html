Everything measures up to the goodness of God. Lewis was sitting on his porch thinking about the goodness of God, the mercy of God, the love of God, the grace of God — and what this world would be if God took those away. Thank the Lord, He makes a way, and that way is Jesus. Then Lewis prays for you: the one laying in bed who can't get up, the one in the hospital bed, the addict seeking hope and direction, and the one who doesn't know Him yet. If that is you today, this prayer is for you.

Sitting Here Thinking About the Goodness of God

Hey y'all, I was just sitting here, thinking again about, you know, the goodness of God, the mercy of God, love of God, grace of God. What if one of those didn't exist? What if God just took those away? You know, this world is in chaos as it is. You know, imagine if God wasn't even in the picture altogether, you know, there's going to come a time where that's going to happen. His wrath is going to be poured out, like the Word says, you know, but thank the Lord that He makes a way, you know — the way is Jesus. Thank the Lord for His love, thank the Lord for His goodness, thank the Lord for His mercy, thank the Lord for His grace, thank the Lord for who He is, His kindness, His sovereign will, there's so many things, so many words that describe who God is. But one word I can always remember — love. He's just love, you know, and if that same Spirit is in you, then you have the ability to show the love of Christ to others, to reflect Jesus. So I encourage you to look to Him always, the author and finisher of your faith, and all true faith is in Him. Everything else does not matter. Everything measures up to the goodness of God.

So I'll pray. Father God, I pray that You would just touch those who are seeking answers, the one who's laying in the bed right now that just can't get up, Lord, I pray that You would send Your Holy Spirit to comfort them, to guide them, to help them in their time of need, the one in the hospital bed, or sick, Lord, and is also struggling with, you know, the what-ifs, Lord, and the addict that's seeking hope, that just needs direction, Lord, I pray that You would send someone their way, Lord, to encourage them, to help them, to lead them the way, to be the hands and feet of You, Lord, and may they do according to what Your Word says, Lord. Give them peace of mind, Lord, and the one who doesn't know You, Lord — Lord, I pray, Father, that the Holy Spirit will send conviction their way, Lord, and may You draw them to Yourself, Lord, and lift them up out of that pit, Lord. Lord, You are the way, Jesus, and You are the life, Lord, and You know all things good, Lord, and nothing else outside of You matters. So thank You, Father, for Your love, Your mercy, and Your grace. Thank You, Father, for healing. Thank You, Father, for Your saving power, Lord. And we pray, Father, that You would just go before us, and we pray, Father, that You would lead us and direct us, Lord, in everything we do, and everything we say, in Jesus' name, amen.

God bless you. May the Lord cause His face to shine on you, and give you peace, in Jesus' name, amen.
