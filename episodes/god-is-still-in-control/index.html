God is still in control. Lewis is back after the whole household was sick — three out of four — and his first words are thank you: thank you for your prayers. Then he prays for you. God promised never to leave us nor forsake us, God cannot lie, all things work together for good for those who love Him, and it was grace that set him free. If you are down and out today, this prayer is for you.

Thank You — and a Prayer for You

I just want to say thank y'all for your prayers. I haven't been putting any videos or podcast episodes here lately because I've been sick. Our household was sick — except one person, three out of four. But you know, God's still good. He's still in control. And even when we're down and out, He promised never to leave us nor forsake us. You know, that's one of His promises, and all of His promises stay true, you know, because God cannot lie. And once again I want to thank you, and I also want to pray for you.

Lord, thank You, Father, for Your love, Your mercy and Your grace. Thank You, Father, for who You are, and what You're doing, and what You've done, and what You're going to do, Lord. Lord, You are sovereign, Lord. Nothing takes You by surprise. Lord, You are in control. You hold everything in Your hands. Lord, like Romans 8:28 says: and we know that all things work together for good to those who love God, to those who are called according to His purpose. Lord, we thank You, Father, for Your Word. We thank You, Father, for Your truth. We thank You, Father, for Your grace, Lord. Lord, yeah, it was grace that set me free, Lord, and I thank You, Father, for that grace. Lord, Your Word says whom the Son sets free shall be free indeed. Thank You, Father, for the freedom You have given me, Lord, that I can point others to You, Lord, because that's what it's all about — reflecting Jesus. And Lord, just proclaiming the gospel of Jesus Christ to a lost and dying world, and to those who are down and out, Lord, to give them encouragement, to lift them up, to show them there is one way, and that way is Jesus. And thank You, Father, for the finished work of the cross. I love You, Lord, and I thank You. We praise You, Father. In Jesus' name, amen.

You know, sometimes I have no words for how good God is, and the really only thing I can say is: He's good. Hallelujah. God bless.
