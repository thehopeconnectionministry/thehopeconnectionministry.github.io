A prayer for your Saturday morning. Lewis opens his Saturday in prayer — for the one who can't quite get out of bed after a rough week, for the addict, for the struggling single mother and the single father trying to make ends meet, and for the family that is broken. God is there in the details, in the struggles, and in the heavy weights people carry — and He is for you, not against you.

The Prayer

Most gracious Heavenly Father, Lord, thank You for Your love, Your mercy and Your grace. Lord, thank You, Father, for who You are, Lord.

Lord, as I pray, Lord, I stand here on this Saturday morning, Lord, just being thankful and grateful.

And Lord, for the one who can't quite get out of bed this morning, Lord, the one who had a rough week, Lord — it was a tough week for, I know, most people. But Lord, You are there in the details, Lord. You are there in the struggles. You are there in the heavy weights that people carry, Lord.

You said for us to cast our cares on You, cast our burdens on You, and You will help us with those weights, Lord. You took it all on the cross, Lord, for a dying world, Lord — You did it for us.

You made a way through Jesus, Father, Lord. You continue to make a way each and every day, each and every moment, Lord. You are always there. You said You would never leave us nor forsake us.

So Lord, I pray, Father, You just touch those who need a touch, Lord. Whether it be physically, emotionally, spiritually, Lord, I pray, Father, that You, the Holy Spirit, would intervene, Lord, to give them comfort, give them grace, mercy, Lord, and show them Your love in a mighty way, Lord.

To the addict, Lord, I pray, Father, You just, Lord, intervene in a mighty way, Lord, that You will show up, and they will see the hope in You, the love in You, the grace in You, Lord. And You are faithful from the beginning to the end, Lord.

For the struggling mother, Lord, who is trying to make ends meet, Lord — the single struggling mother, Lord, that day by day, moment by moment, Lord, they seem hopeless — Lord, may You provide them hope through Your Son, Lord. May they see that in You, Lord.

And Lord, for that father, the single father, Lord, that is struggling, Lord, that is trying to make ends meet, Lord, to provide for his kids, Lord.

Lord, for that family, Lord — the mother and father, Lord — that the family is broken, Lord, may You mend that family, Lord, now, Lord, in the name of Jesus, according to Your will, according to Your glory, Lord.

We lift You up, Lord, and magnify You, Lord. Lord, everything we say and everything we do, may we honor You, and may we always be grateful.

Lord, we hear all these prayers, Lord, and You know each and every one. I pray, Lord, You just godly direct, Lord. May Your Holy Spirit flow through all these situations, Lord.

We give You all the glory, all the honor, all the praise. In Jesus' name we pray. Amen.

Woo! God's good. I love you. God bless you. God's for you, and not against you.
