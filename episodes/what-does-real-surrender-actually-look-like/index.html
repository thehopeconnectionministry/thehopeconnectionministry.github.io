What Does Real Surrender Actually Look Like?

Surrender isn't giving up. It's giving over.

  • Surrender
  • Faith
  • Freedom
  • Recovery
  • Hope

"Father, if you are willing, remove this cup from me. Nevertheless, not my will, but yours, be done."

Luke 22:42 (ESV)

For thirty years I held on to everything — and it nearly killed me. Then I watched Jesus in the garden pray, "Not my will, but yours, be done" (Luke 22:42), and I finally learned what surrender actually looks like.

Why we fight surrender for decades

Most of us hear the word "surrender" and think it means losing. Giving up. Waving the white flag on our own lives. So we grip tighter — our plans, our pain, our way of doing things — even while the grip is what's destroying us. In this episode we talk honestly about why we fight it, and why the fight itself is the proof that we need it.

The cost of holding on — and the freedom of open hands

What does it really cost to keep holding on? Everything it cost me for thirty years. And what's waiting on the other side of open hands? Freedom — the kind you can't manufacture and can't earn. The Greek word for what Jesus did is paradidōmi — to hand over. That's what Jesus did for us. That's what He asks of us.
