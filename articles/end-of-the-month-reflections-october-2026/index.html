End of the Month Reflections — October 2026: The Month in Review

Look back honestly. Then look up expectantly.

  • Reflection
  • Hope
  • Faith
  • Gratitude
Look back honestly. Then look up expectantly.

October is in the books. Time for a month-end review — sit down, look back over the whole month, and tell the truth about it: the hard parts and the mercy, in that order.

The hard side, first. Some doors stayed shut all month. One platform banned the account mid-month and still hasn't opened it back up. Some plans didn't move an inch. If I only counted what went wrong, I could fill a page — but a review tells the whole story, not half of it.

What God did in October:

  • The podcast found its new home. The Hope Connection moved to Spotify for Creators, all our episodes carried over — nine now live.
  • Rachel told her story. Her testimony episode, "My Story," went out October 13, and people are still writing about it.
  • Episode 5 published. "Surrender, Part 1: Not Giving Up — Giving Over" (Luke 22:42) went live October 27, and the rest of the Surrender series is scripted and coming.
  • The book's audiobook is finished. Hope Met Me Where I Was — 2 hours 43 minutes, complete. The book itself gets revealed December 16.
  • The walk films kept walking. Short movies for Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, and Deuteronomy joined Ezekiel on YouTube — one for every book of the Bible, working our way through all 66.
  • Night Anchor showed up every night. Night after night of anchors for the dark hours — Day 15 alone was watched over 900 times in the last week of the month.
  • Teaching got built for months ahead. The Mountains and Valleys series (72 entries), the Temple comic, the Fruit of the Spirit series, plus Faith and Trust the Process episode scripts — all written and ready to roll out.
  • 50 Days of Hope launched on October 28, counting up to the book reveal on December 16.

Where we stand as November opens: 295 followers on the Facebook Page, 142 subscribers on YouTube, 23 members in our Facebook community praying for each other every week, and 12.9 thousand monthly views on Pinterest. Small numbers by the world's math. Every one of them is a person.

None of this happened because I'm something special. It happened because "the steadfast love of the LORD never ceases; his mercies never come to an end; they are new every morning" (Lamentations 3:22-23, ESV).

That's the review, friend. October's mercies are spent, and November's are already on the way. Look back honestly. Then look up expectantly.

What did God do in your October? I'd love to hear it in the comments.

Christ Proclaimed. Jesus is Lord.
— Lewis
