An Honor I Didn't Take Lightly

This past week, I had the honor of being a guest on SHOUT OUT TO GOD LIVE with Tray Crayton. I shared my story — the parts that are easy to tell and the parts that aren't. Here's what I want you to take away from it.

Searching, Even as a Child

I was raised in church. My grandmother took me. I remember sitting in a holiness church as a boy, hearing people speak in tongues, and asking my grandmother what they were saying. Even then, I was searching — trying to understand what was happening around me.

The Outsider Feeling

As I grew, I carried a feeling that I didn't belong — like I was different from everybody else, and the only one who felt that way. That feeling of being on the outside turned into a long inner battle. If you've ever felt like you don't fit in, like you're the only one going through what you're going through — I understand. And you're not alone.

Performing Faith

Here's the honest confession: a lot of what I did in church as a teenager — the camps, the choir — some of it was about being seen. "Hey, look at me." There's a difference between doing things for God and doing things to be seen doing things for God. God knows the difference, even when we fool everybody else.

He Never Walked Away

Through the searching, the performing, the walking away into addiction — God never walked away from me.

"I will never leave you nor forsake you." — Hebrews 13:5 (ESV)

It doesn't matter where you've been or how far you've run. He's right there, waiting for you to turn around.
