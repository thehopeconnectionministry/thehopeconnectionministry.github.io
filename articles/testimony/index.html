Thirty Years Lost

For more than thirty years, I was trapped in drug addiction. I tried the rehabs. I tried the meetings. Nothing worked. I knew about God — I'd been raised in church — but I didn't know Him. There's a difference, and that difference is everything.

The Night Everything Changed

On my last stay in a men's discipleship program, I was reading John 17 — and two verses stopped me cold:

"And this is eternal life, that they know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom you have sent." — John 17:3 (ESV)

Eternal life isn't a place. It's a Person. It's knowing God. That night, I believed God's Word was true — and I knew God had changed my life, right then and there.

You Are Not Alone

If you're in the middle of your own battle — addiction, shame, a past you can't outrun — hear this: your past is not too far for His grace to reach. Jesus met me where I was. He'll meet you too.

"Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come." — 2 Corinthians 5:17 (ESV)
